About CivicNature

CivicNature is a civic reporting platform empowering Sulaymaniyah citizens to document, report, and track urban and environmental issues — from broken roads to illegal tree cutting — until they're resolved.

Problem Background

Sulaymaniyah, like many growing cities, faces persistent challenges: potholes, failed street lighting, drainage failures, illegal waste dumping, and the quiet destruction of urban green spaces — parks left to deteriorate, trees cut without authorisation, public gardens neglected.

The core problem is not just the damage itself, but the absence of a transparent, structured channel for citizens to report these issues and hold authorities accountable. Reports get made informally, then lost. Problems persist for months.

SDG Alignment

🏙️ SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities

CivicNature directly supports SDG 11 by enabling participatory urban governance. Citizens can report and track infrastructure and environmental issues publicly, creating accountability and community-driven pressure for resolution. The inclusion of green space and environmental damage reporting also supports the SDG 11 target of providing universal access to safe, inclusive, and accessible green and public spaces.

Our Solution

CivicNature is a web-based civic reporting platform built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Citizens submit photo-supported reports, the community upvotes urgent issues, and every report's resolution status is tracked publicly — with no login or barriers to entry.

Key Features

📸 Photo Reporting

Attach a photo from your camera or gallery — visual proof makes reports impossible to dismiss.

📊 Public Dashboard

All reports visible to everyone. Transparent, filterable by category, community-owned.

👍 Community Upvoting

Citizens signal which issues matter most. Urgency rises from the community itself.

🌿 Green Space Coverage

Report environmental and nature-related damage, not just infrastructure.
